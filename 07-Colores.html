Shades of gray

By using equal values for red, green, and blue, you will get different shades of gray:

rgb(60, 60, 60)

rgb(90, 90, 90)

rgb(120, 120, 120)

rgb(180, 180, 180)

rgb(210, 210, 210)

rgb(240, 240, 240)

Make transparent colors with RGBA

rgba(255, 99, 71, 0)

rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.2)

rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.4)

rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.6)

rgba(255, 99, 71, 0.8)

rgba(255, 99, 71, 1)

HSL Lightness

The third parameter of hsl() defines the lightness. 0% means black, and 100% means white:

hsl(0, 100%, 0%)

hsl(0, 100%, 25%)

hsl(0, 100%, 50%)

hsl(0, 100%, 75%)

hsl(0, 100%, 90%)

hsl(0, 100%, 100%)
